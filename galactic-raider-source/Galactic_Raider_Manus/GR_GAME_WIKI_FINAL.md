## 1. What Is Galactic Raider?

Galactic Raider is a financial empire simulator. You start with **$1,000,000** and your goal is to grow it into a multi-billion dollar portfolio by investing across stocks, bonds, forex, commodities, crypto, ETFs, and eventually the entire solar system.

The game runs in real time — one turn per minute on the live server. Each turn the markets move, dividends pay, taxes are deducted, and CEO decisions arrive. You decide when to buy, when to sell, when to donate, and when to spin the Wheel of Fortune.

**This is not gambling.** Every mechanic mirrors how real financial markets work. The game is designed to teach you.

## 2. Getting Started — Your First 10 Turns

**Turn 1 — Check your wallets**
You start with:
- 💵 Cash Wallet: $100,000
- 🏦 Savings Wallet: $100,000
- ⚡ Trading Wallet: $800,000

Your Trading Wallet is your investment capital. All buying and selling happens from here.

**Turn 1 — Read the Tax Era**
Check the current tax era at the top of the Home screen. If you are in **Capital Gains** or **Low Tax** era, this is an ideal time to be active. If you are in **High Tax** era, consider holding.

**Turns 1–5 — Make your first investment**
Go to Markets → Stocks. Sort by **Analyst Rating**. The green STRONG BUY companies have the highest analyst consensus. Tap a company to read the founder story, see analyst opinions, and understand what the business does.

Buy a small position — try 1,000 shares of a company you understand. Watch the price move each turn.

**Turn 5 — Consider a bond**
Bonds are safer than stocks. Go to Bonds and buy 5–10 US Treasury bonds. They pay quarterly coupons and protect your capital.

**Turn 10 — Check your portfolio**
Go to Portfolio. You should now see your stock holding, its P&L (profit and loss), and your available cash. If you are in profit, hold. If you are in loss, assess whether the company fundamentals changed or if it is just market noise.

## 3. The 3 Wallets — Understanding Your Money

This is the most important concept in the game. You have three separate wallets and they serve different purposes.

### 💵 Cash Wallet
- Day-to-day spending money
- Used for tax payments, loan repayments, Foundation fees
- NOT automatically liquidated in bankruptcy
- Earns no interest
- **Rule:** Keep at least $10,000 here at all times

### 🏦 Savings Wallet
- Long-term protected funds
- Earns **2% per year** credited every turn
- **NOT protected by default** — you must open a Foundation first
- Once Foundation is open, savings can never be touched in bankruptcy
- Dividends from stocks are credited here
- **Rule:** Open a Foundation as soon as you have $10,000 to spare

### ⚡ Trading Wallet
- All investment activity happens here
- Stocks, bonds, forex, commodities, crypto, ETFs — all draw from here
- Auto-buys draw from here only
- **First wallet liquidated in bankruptcy**
- Earns no interest
- **Rule:** Never put money you cannot afford to lose in Trading Wallet

### Transferring Between Wallets
Use the Transfer tab on the Home screen. Choose your direction and select a percentage:
- **10%** — small move
- **25%** — quarter
- **50%** — half
- **100%** — everything available (leaves decimal remainder in source)

**Trading → Cash:** 1 turn settlement delay
**Cash → Trading:** Instant
**Savings transfers:** 1 turn settle going out, instant coming in

### Opening a Foundation
Go to Wallets → tap "Open Foundation". Costs **$10,000 from Cash Wallet**. Once open, your Savings Wallet is permanently protected from bankruptcy for the rest of the game. Worth doing early.

## 4. Earth Markets

### 📈 Stocks
15 companies across 10 sectors. Each company has:
- A **founder story** — who built it and how
- **3–5 analyst opinions** from named research firms
- **Analyst consensus** — STRONG BUY / BUY / HOLD / SELL
- **Price target** — where analysts think the price is going
- **P/E ratio** — bounded by the Economic Governor

**How to pick a stock:**
1. Sort by Analyst Rating — start with STRONG BUY
2. Read the company story — do you understand the business?
3. Check the P/E ratio — is it near the bottom of its range? (more upside)
4. Check the dividend yield — income investors look for 3%+
5. Check the beta — high beta (1.5+) means more volatile, higher potential gains and losses

**Sectors and their characteristics:**
- **Technology** — high growth, high P/E (15–35×), volatile
- **Banking** — stable, lower P/E (8–15×), reliable dividends
- **Mining** — cyclical, low P/E (6–12×), commodity price sensitive
- **Healthcare** — defensive, high P/E (12–35×), pipeline risk
- **Utilities** — very stable, 4%+ dividend, almost no growth
- **Energy** — oil price sensitive, medium P/E (8–20×)

**Buying:**
Tap a company → Buy button → choose quantity → Confirm. Auto-sim pauses during purchase.

**Selling:**
Your profit is taxed as Capital Gains at the current era rate. Selling at a loss pays zero CGT.

### 📋 Bonds
Five bond types from AAA (safest) to B (riskiest). Pay quarterly coupons.
- **US Treasury 10Y** — 4.5% coupon, AAA rated, safest
- **African Govt Bond** — 12.5% coupon, BB rated, higher risk
- **Emerging Mkt Bond** — 14.8% coupon, B rated, highest risk

Bond price = Face Value × (Coupon ÷ Current Yield) × Rating Multiplier. When yields rise, bond prices fall. When yields fall, prices rise.

**Strategy:** Buy high-yield bonds (BB/B) when you can afford the risk. Buy AAA when you want safety.

### 💱 Forex
Trade currency pairs. Long = you profit if the price rises. Short = you profit if the price falls.
- **No position limits** — you can put your entire Trading Wallet on one forex trade
- USD/AED and USD/CNY are locked (spec requirement — these are fixed rates)
- P&L updates every turn

**Forex strategy:** High risk, high reward. Use as a hedge — if you think markets will fall, open a Short position to profit from the decline.

### ⛽ Commodities
Oil, Gold, Silver, Gas, Corn, Wheat, Copper, Lithium. All mean-revert to their base prices over time, so extreme price moves eventually correct.

**Gold** is a classic safe haven — tends to rise when markets fall.
**Lithium** is tied to EV demand — long-term structural growth.

### ₿ Crypto
Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, BNB. Higher volatility than commodities (±8% per turn). Same mean-reversion prevents total collapse.

## 5. Tax Eras — The Most Important Game Mechanic

The game rotates through **8 tax eras**, each lasting **60 turns**. The sequence is random each new game.

| Era | CGT | Dividend Tax | Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Normal** | 20% | 15% | Standard play |
| **High Tax** | 30% | 25% | Hold — don't realise gains |
| **Low Tax** | 10% | 5% | Buy and sell freely |
| **Capital Gains** | 5% | 15% | SELL profitable positions now |
| **Dividend** | 20% | 5% | Buy high-yield stocks |
| **Transaction** | 20% | 15% | Trade frequently — txn cost near zero |
| **Wealth Tax** | 20% | 15% | Donate to reduce — affects $10B+ portfolios |
| **Normal** | 20% | 15% | Cycle repeats |

**Key rule:** Never sell a profit in High Tax era if you can wait for Capital Gains era. The difference on $1M profit: you keep $950K in Capital Gains vs $700K in High Tax. That is $250K difference on one trade.

**Tax prepayment:** If you are in Capital Gains era (5% CGT), you can prepay your annual tax at that rate — locking it in even if the era changes next turn.

## 6. CEO Decisions

Every 30 turns, companies you own 10%+ in will send you a decision to make.

### How it works
1. A blue banner appears at the top of the screen — **do not ignore it**
2. Tap the banner to see the full decision
3. Read the context, all options, and their consequences
4. Choose within **10 turns** or the worst option is auto-applied and the CEO's reputation drops

### Decision types
- **Acquisition** — buy another company (adds debt, adds growth)
- **Dividend policy** — raise, maintain or cut the dividend
- **Expansion** — enter a new market (high risk, high reward)
- **Cost restructuring** — cut jobs to improve margins (reputation risk)
- **CEO replacement** — only available at 50%+ ownership

### Board access tiers
| Ownership | Rights |
|---|---|
| **10%+** | Vote on dividends, advance notice of decisions |
| **25%+** | Propose strategy, veto acquisitions, nominate directors |
| **50%+** | Replace CEO, declare special dividends, force asset sales |

**CEO reputation** affects how often they bring good opportunities. A CEO at 80/100 rep brings better deals than one at 40/100.

## 7. The Solar System — 7 Planets

Unlock the Solar System by meeting 5 criteria:
1. Net Worth $5 billion
2. Reach Turn 300
3. Hold stocks from 3+ regions
4. Hold 2+ bond types
5. Make 2+ donations

Once unlocked, 7 planets open with their own companies, currencies and economies.

### Planet Guide

**🔴 Mars — Mining Economy**
Currency: MCR (1 MCR = $0.85 USD)
Industries: Lithium, iron ore, rare earths
Contagion: Earth GDP changes hit Mars after 2 turns at 50% strength
Strategy: Buy mining companies during Earth downturns — Mars recovers faster

**🟡 Venus — Energy Economy**
Currency: VCR (1 VCR = $0.75 USD)
Industries: Solar energy, atmospheric chemical processing
Fully automated. High margins. Contagion from Earth in 2 turns at 67% strength.
Strategy: Long-term income — high dividend yields from automated factories

**🟠 Jupiter — Robotic Economy**
Currency: JCR (1 JCR = $0.90 USD)
Industries: Deep atmosphere mining, AI research, fusion energy
**Storm system:** Every 50–100 turns a catastrophic storm hits. Production falls 30%. JCR crashes.
**Recovery:** Mutual Space Council activates — other planets send raw materials. Jupiter rebuilds in 15–20 turns then booms.
**Strategy:** Buy Jupiter companies immediately after a storm. They are at their cheapest and about to recover strongly.

**🪐 Saturn — Ring Mining Economy**
Currency: STC (1 STC = $0.70 USD)
Industries: Ryzolith ore (rarest material in solar system), ice water exports
Ryzolith is used in quantum computing. Saturn has a monopoly on its supply.
Strategy: Long-term hold. Ryzolith demand grows every 100 turns.

**☿ Mercury — Solar Energy Economy**
Currency: MRC (1 MRC = $0.60 USD)
Industries: Solar arrays, thermoelectric mining, extreme-environment robotics
Closest to the Sun — 24× Earth solar intensity. Day 430°C, night −180°C. Fully automated.
Strategy: Best solar returns in the game. Steady income, low volatility.

**🔵 Uranus — Ice Mining Economy**
Currency: URU (1 URU = $0.55 USD)
Industries: Methane/ammonia ice, cryogenic technology, extreme-cold research
42-year seasons mean investment cycles are decades long in game time.
Strategy: Very long-term hold. Low volatility. Best for income investors.

**💜 Neptune — Deep Research Economy**
Currency: NPT (1 NPT = $0.50 USD)
Industries: Advanced physics research, wind energy, unique deep-space minerals
2,100 km/h winds. Furthest from Sun. Highest risk, highest potential.
Strategy: Speculative. Treat like crypto — small position, massive upside if research succeeds.

### Cross-Dimension Transfers
Go to Transfer tab. Choose source and destination planet. Select 10/25/50/100% of your wallet. A confirmation popup shows:
- Exact amount sent
- 0.5% transfer fee
- Destination era tax applied silently
- Exact amount received in destination currency

Transfer history is logged for your records.

### Economic Contagion
When Earth GDP rises or falls, other planets are affected — but with a delay and reduced intensity:
- Mars: 2-turn delay, 50% of Earth's change
- Venus: 2-turn delay, 67% of Earth's change
- Jupiter: 3-turn delay, 83% of Earth's change
- Saturn: 4-turn delay, 45% of Earth's change
- Mercury: 2-turn delay, 55% of Earth's change
- Uranus: 5-turn delay, 35% of Earth's change
- Neptune: 6-turn delay, 28% of Earth's change

**Diversification tip:** Invest across multiple planets. When Earth falls, Jupiter is still rising (for 3 more turns). This is why diversification across dimensions reduces risk.

## 8. ETFs and IPOs

### ETFs — Exchange Traded Funds
ETFs let you invest in a basket of companies with one purchase. Lower risk than individual stocks.

Five available ETFs:
| ETF | Type | Expense Ratio | Dividend | Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Global Equity | Broad market | 0.12% | 1.8% | Core long-term holding |
| Tech Focus | Sector | 0.25% | 0.4% | Growth play |
| Dividend Income | Income | 0.18% | 4.8% | Income investors |
| Mining & Resources | Sector | 0.32% | 0.9% | Commodity cycle play |
| Planet Gateway | Thematic | 0.45% | 0.2% | Space economy exposure |

Check the Sharpe Ratio — above 1.0 is good, above 1.5 is excellent. Max Drawdown shows the worst loss you could have experienced — lower is safer.

### IPOs — Initial Public Offerings
New companies list periodically. You can book shares before listing.

**Reading IPO demand:**
- Over 5× oversubscribed — strong institutional demand, likely to list above midpoint
- 2–5× oversubscribed — healthy demand, fair listing expected
- Under 1× — weak demand, may list below midpoint or fail

**Allocation:** In oversubscribed IPOs, you typically receive 85% of what you booked. In undersubscribed IPOs you may receive your full booking.

**Post-listing:** Shares often move significantly on listing day. If oversubscribed, expect a pop above IPO price. Undersubscribed IPOs often fall.

## 9. Philanthropy — Redemption Points and the Wheel of Fortune

### Why Donate?
Three reasons:
1. **Tax relief** — donations reduce your tax rate on all income for multiple turns
2. **Redemption points** — convert to Wheel of Fortune spin tokens
3. **Moral standing** — affects how other players and the game world perceive you

### Donation Categories
| Category | Tax Relief | Duration | Point Multiplier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Healthcare | 20% | 3 turns | 1.3× |
| Education | 25% | 5 turns | **1.5×** (highest) |
| Environment | **30%** | **7 turns** (longest) | 1.1× |
| Infrastructure | 15% | 4 turns | 1.0× |
| Poverty | 20% | 3 turns | 1.2× |
| Science | 25% | 5 turns | 1.0× |
| Arts | 10% | 2 turns | 1.0× |
| Disaster Relief | **35%** (highest) | **8 turns** (longest) | 1.0× |

Minimum donation: **$1,000,000**

Tax relief stacks — donate to two categories and get combined relief up to 75% maximum.

### Redemption Points
- $1M donation = base 1,000 points × multiplier
- Points cap at 5,000 total
- Points decay 1% every 100 turns — use them
- 500 points = 1 Wheel of Fortune spin token (after 2+ donations)

### The Wheel of Fortune
**Requirements to spin:**
- 500+ redemption points
- 1+ spin token
- Under 5 lifetime spins
- 2+ total donations made
- 100+ turns since last spin

**Cost:** 500 points + 1 token per spin

**Outcomes:**
| Result | Probability |
|---|---|
| 5% debt forgiveness | 30% (most common) |
| 10% debt forgiveness | 20% |
| 15% debt forgiveness | 15% |
| 25% debt forgiveness | 10% |
| 50% debt forgiveness — JACKPOT | 10% |
| Bonus 100–200 points (consolation) | 15% |

## 10. The Global Sovereign Fund (GSF)

The GSF is your third dedicated investment wallet — separate from Cash, Savings and Trading.

- **Minimum deposit:** $50,000
- **Deposit fee:** 2% deducted on entry
- **Return:** GSF rate ÷ 365 credited to your Trading Wallet every single turn
- **Current rate:** Fluctuates between 3–14% annually
- **Withdraw:** Anytime, full balance, to Trading Wallet

**Example:** $1,000,000 in GSF at 12% annual rate = $328.77 per turn credited automatically.

**Strategy:** Deposit a large amount early. The daily compounding adds up significantly over hundreds of turns. This is your passive income engine.

## 11. Bankruptcy and Recovery

### Bankruptcy Stages
**Yellow Alert — Net worth below $100,000**
You have time. Reduce positions. Transfer from Personal to Trading to stabilise.

**Orange Alert — Net worth goes negative**
Forced liquidation begins. 10% of your Trading Wallet is sold each turn. You cannot buy new positions. Transfer from Savings/Cash to Trading Wallet to recover.

**Red — Net worth below −$500,000**
BANKRUPT. Game over for this session. Your Academy progress and Foundation balance are retained.

### Liquidation Order
1. Trading Wallet — sold first, 10% per turn
2. Cash Wallet — sold if Trading is empty
3. Savings Wallet — **ONLY if you have no Foundation**
4. Foundation balance — **NEVER touched**

### Recovery Strategy
1. Open Foundation early to protect Savings
2. Keep Cash Wallet funded as a buffer
3. Use the Wheel of Fortune — up to 50% debt forgiveness per spin
4. Use the 7-day login streak — 10% debt forgiveness free
5. Donate to Disaster Relief (35% tax relief) to maximise income while recovering

## 12. The 7-Day Login Streak

Log in every day for 7 consecutive days.

**Rewards:**
- ✅ 10% of your current debt is automatically forgiven
- ✅ 1 free Wheel of Fortune spin token

**Extended streaks:**
- 14 days: 20% debt forgiveness + 2 spin tokens

Missing one day resets your streak to zero.

## 14. Advanced Strategies

### The Tax Timing Strategy
Track which era you are in and how many turns are left. If you have a large profit and Capital Gains era is 5 turns away, wait. That patience could save you 25% in tax on a $10M position = $2.5M saved.

### The Jupiter Storm Strategy
Watch Jupiter GDP. When a storm event fires, Jupiter companies drop 30%+. Buy immediately after the storm is announced. The Mutual Space Council activates and Jupiter rebounds within 20 turns. This is the highest potential short-term trade in the game.

### The Foundation + Philanthropy Shield
Open Foundation early. Donate to Environment or Disaster Relief for 30–35% tax relief. Your effective CGT rate in Capital Gains era becomes 5% × 0.65 = 3.25%. You keep 96.75% of every profitable sale. This is the closest the game gets to a legal tax shelter.

### The GSF Compound Strategy
Put 50–60% of your net worth into GSF at high rates (10%+). Let it compound daily. At $100M with a 12% rate, you earn $32,876 per turn passively. After 1,000 turns that is $32.8M of passive income without touching a single investment.

### The Diversification Matrix
Hold positions across Earth stocks, bonds, planet currencies, ETFs and GSF simultaneously. When one asset class falls, others typically hold or rise. The optimal allocation at $10M+ net worth:
- 30% Earth stocks (across 3+ sectors)
- 20% Planet investments (2+ planets)
- 20% GSF
- 15% Bonds (mix of ratings)
- 10% ETFs
- 5% Speculative (crypto, Neptune, high-yield bonds)

## 15. Glossary

**AUM** — Assets Under Management. Total value of all money in an ETF.

**Beta** — Measure of volatility vs the market. Beta 1.0 = moves with market. Beta 2.0 = twice as volatile. Beta 0.5 = half as volatile.

**Capital Gains Tax (CGT)** — Tax on profit when you sell an asset. Only on profit, never on your original investment.

**Contagion** — When one planet's economy affects another. Always delayed and reduced in intensity.

**Coupon** — The annual interest payment from a bond, expressed as % of face value.

**Dividend** — Quarterly cash payment from a company's profits to shareholders.

**EPS** — Earnings Per Share. Profit divided by shares outstanding. This is fixed in Galactic Raider — the Governor uses it to keep prices realistic.

**ETF** — Exchange Traded Fund. A basket of multiple companies in one investment.

**Foundation** — A legal structure that permanently protects your Savings Wallet from bankruptcy.

**GSF** — Global Sovereign Fund. Your passive income wallet earning daily interest.

**IPO** — Initial Public Offering. A private company listing on the stock market for the first time.

**JCR** — Jupiter Credits. Jupiter's currency.

**MCR** — Mars Credits. Mars currency.

**Mutual Space Council (MSC)** — Automatic interplanetary support system that activates when Jupiter suffers a storm. Other planets send raw materials automatically.

**NPT** — Neptune Credits.

**P/E Ratio** — Price divided by Earnings Per Share. The Economic Governor keeps this within industry-specific bounds.

**Redemption Points** — Earned through donations. Convert to Wheel of Fortune spin tokens.

**Ryzolith** — Rarest material in the solar system, found only on Saturn. Used in quantum computing.

**Sharpe Ratio** — Risk-adjusted return. Above 1.0 is good. Above 1.5 is excellent. Shows return relative to risk taken.

**STC** — Saturn Credits.

**URU** — Uranus Credits.

**VCR** — Venus Credits.

**Wealth Tax** — Automatic tax of 0.1% per turn on net worth above $10 billion. Cannot be avoided — can only be reduced through philanthropy.

**Yield** — Income from an investment as a percentage of its price. Bond yield rises when bond price falls.
