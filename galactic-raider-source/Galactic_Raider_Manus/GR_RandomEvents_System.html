Three-Type Event System
Random events fire throughout gameplay based on probability, economic conditions, and player portfolio state. They teach real investing lessons — the market is never fully predictable.
🌊
Type 1 — Market Events
10 Events
Affect the entire economy. Hit all players simultaneously. No one controls them. Range from rate surprises to geopolitical crises to global recessions.
🏢
Type 2 — Company Events
10 Events
Affect specific companies you hold. Controlling stake gives you influence over outcomes. Ownership tier determines your level of control.
⭐
Type 3A — Market Birth
10 Events
Create something entirely new in the economy. New regions, sectors, currencies, exchanges. Permanent additions to the game world. Early movers win.
🧬
Type 3B — Company Birth
10 Events
Create new entities from existing companies. Spinoffs, IPOs, mergers, joint ventures. Players holding parent companies benefit from births.

⚖️ Governor Constraint — Non-Negotiable
All events operate within Economic Governor bounds. No event can push a stock below P/E 5x or above P/E 50x. No event can make net income exceed revenue. No event can make T-Bills negative. Events create volatility within the guardrails — never outside them.

Governor Constraints on Events
The Economic Governor runs first every turn — before any event resolves. Events create volatility within the Governor's guardrails, never outside them.
P/E Bounds — 5x to 50x
A positive event cannot push a stock above 50x P/E. A negative event cannot push a stock below 5x P/E. If an event would breach these bounds, the Governor clamps the price before it resolves.
// Event attempts +40% price surge on stock at P/E 38
// New price would imply P/E 53.2 → Governor clamps to P/E 50
// Actual surge limited to +31.6% to maintain Governor ceiling
Net Income Rule — Cannot Exceed Revenue
A positive event that boosts company revenue cannot accidentally inflate net income above revenue. The Governor enforces Math.min(netIncome, revenue) after every event resolution.
T-Bills Floor — Hard Zero
A negative event that drains company cash cannot push T-Bills below zero. The floor is absolute. Events that create cash shortfalls instead push companies into credit line territory.
Research Exemption — Jupiter, Neptune, Uranus
Research companies on these three planets may have negative net income (up to -50% of revenue). Events can push them further negative within this limit but not beyond. All other Governor rules still apply.
Daily Price Change Limit — ±8% Per Turn
No single event can move a stock more than ±8% in one turn. A large event (e.g. fraud investigation) that would cause -40% resolves over multiple turns — typically 5–15 turns — each within the ±8% daily limit.
// Fraud event: total impact -35% over 8 turns
// Turn 1: -8% (Governor limit) → Turn 2: -8% → Turn 3: -7%
// Turns 4–8: gradual decline → Total: -35% over event duration
