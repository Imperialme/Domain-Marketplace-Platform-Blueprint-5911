# Spare Parts Brand — Naming Brief (Pakistan)

Date: 2026-07-18 · Availability checked live (Vercel domain API for .com, DNS probes for .pk)

## Goal
A standalone consumer brand for spare parts in Pakistan — separate from spareparts.me,
built through influencer marketing, and positioned to onboard Chinese/emerging OEM and
aftermarket brands.

## Recommendation (ranked)

| Rank | Name | .com | .pk (DNS signal) | Notes |
|------|------|------|------------------|-------|
| 1 | **KalPurza** | ✅ available ($11.25/yr) | ✅ likely available | "Kal purza" is the everyday Urdu idiom for machine parts. Instantly understood, memorable, made for spoken influencer content. Covers auto + machinery, OEM + aftermarket. |
| 2 | **PurzaMart** | ✅ available ($11.25/yr) | ✅ likely available | Clear "parts shop" meaning; slightly more generic than KalPurza. |
| 3 | **SpareMandi** | ✅ available ($11.25/yr) | ✅ likely available | Marketplace ("mandi") vibe; good B2B2C feel; shares the word "spare" with spareparts.me. |
| 4 | PurzaHub / MyPurza / PartPur | ✅ available | untested | Fallbacks if the above are rejected. |

## Names evaluated and rejected

- **Parts United / United Parts** — partsunited.com and unitedparts.com both taken; "United"
  is already a major Pakistani auto brand family (United Auto Industries / United Motorcycles),
  so a parts brand under "United" invites confusion and trademark risk.
- **procurement.pk** — B2B/cold, doesn't pair with "parts", hard to use in Urdu video content.
- **Purza (alone)** — purza.com taken; purza.pk registered and resolving to a live server.
- **PartsWala, PartsMandi, PartsBazaar, PartsAdda, GariParts, Purzo, Purzon** — .com taken.

## Action items

   the registration lapsed. If it is re-registrable, recovering it changes this whole picture.
2. Verify .pk availability at pknic.net.pk (DNS absence is a signal, not proof).
3. Register .com + .pk + .com.pk for the chosen name in one sitting.
4. Grab matching Instagram/TikTok/YouTube/Facebook handles the same day.
5. Run a trademark search with IPO Pakistan before spending on branding.

## Strategy notes

- Keep **spareparts.me** untouched as the global/umbrella asset; the Pakistan brand stands alone.
- Influencer-led growth means the name must sound natural *spoken in Urdu* — this is why an
  Urdu-rooted brandable beats an English descriptive name here.
- For Chinese OEM/aftermarket brands, pitch a Daraz-style "official brand store" model; the
  platform brand carries trust locally while their brand carries the product.
